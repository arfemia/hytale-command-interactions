First release. InteractionCommands adds one thing to Hytale: a `RunCommand` interaction step you
can drop inline into any weapon, consumable, or custom block's interaction chain to run a command.

- **New: the `RunCommand` interaction Type.** Author it anywhere a native interaction chain
  accepts a step: a weapon's swing/hit chain, a consumable's Use/Secondary, a custom
  RootInteraction, a custom item or block. Runs one or more commands, either with full server
  console authority (`RunAs: "Server"`, the default) or as the interacting player themself
  (`RunAs: "Player"`, subject to their own permissions).
- **New: per-player cooldowns, chance-gated procs, and permission gates.** `Cooldown` throttles
  repeat fires per player; `Chance` turns a step into a probabilistic proc; `Permission` requires
  the interacting player to hold a node.
- **New: placeholder substitution.** Every command can reference `{player}`, `{uuid}`,
  `{x}`/`{y}`/`{z}`, `{world}`, `{target}`, `{targetUuid}`, and `{item}`, filled in at fire time.
- **New: silent-skip gate semantics.** A cooldown, a chance miss, or a missing permission never
  breaks the chain: the step quietly does nothing and everything around it (the weapon swing, the
  item's consumption) proceeds exactly as if the step weren't there. A command left with an
  unresolvable placeholder (like `{target}` with no target present) is skipped on its own, without
  affecting the rest of the command list.
- **Zero dependencies.** One jar, no companion mods, no content pack required. InteractionCommands
  ships no assets and no lang files of its own; you author `RunCommand` steps in your own pack.
- **New: a runnable example pack.** `InteractionCommandsExamples.zip` (in the repository's
  `examples/` folder) ships three demo items - a blade with a chance-gated on-hit `/give` proc, a
  potion, and a meal that each run `/give` on consume - so you can watch `RunCommand` fire within
  a minute of installing.

See `README.md` and `CURSEFORGE.md` in this mod's repository for the full schema reference and
worked examples.
