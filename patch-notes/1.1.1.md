Command Interactions is built for Hytale Update 7 (0.7.x), with no change to how `RunCommand`
works. A server still on Update 6 stays on 1.1.0.

- Added the mod's own icon to the game's mod list.
- The example pack moves to 1.1.1 with the jar, and its potion uses the game's own drink cooldown.
- On Update 7, a held item's `Ability2` and `Ability3` go to your slotted runes, so a `RunCommand`
  step on either one never runs, and nothing shows in the log. Put the step on `Primary` or
  `Secondary`. `Ability1`, `Ability4` and `Pick` still reach the held item, except for a player
  whose MMO Skill Tree ability slot sits on that input. The README's troubleshooting section has
  the details.
